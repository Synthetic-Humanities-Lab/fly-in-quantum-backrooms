36 qubits, 6 x 6 rooms, 60 couplings, 16 retained measurement records.
Raw retained weights total 1/256; the loader normalizes them to 1/16 each.
Measurements, expectation values, couplings and annotations are preserved.
The active subset is conditioned on the original wall-observation vote mask.
The main mode's AND rule supplies targets to persistent per-wall states.
The displayed wall mosaic is not itself a single hardware measurement.
No live QPU computation or new provider execution occurs.

Floor 1 contains the QPS. Collection, sanity death, hunting and progression
are disabled. The radiation-room screen glow follows the fly and freezes with
the simulation. An independently authored Cleanroom-style entity is present
from startup, outside the opening camera views, and wanders without a target. This requested adaptation
replaces the original QPS-triggered hunt. It cannot harm or collide with the fly.
The fly senses its local silhouette, occluded by nearer walls. Its separate RNG
and movement never observe the maze or hold walls open.
See assets/CLEANROOM-NOTICE.txt for asset provenance and approximation limits.

Continuous fly embodiment applies the original wall-centre observation sector
at the fly's continuous position and heading. Neural feature rays and the
corner map retain separate fields of view. Spectator cameras affect none of
these inputs. A wall cannot become blocking through the occupied fly body;
that narrow collision adapter is separate from the reference-mode simulation.
The fly body is 1% of room width. The map is enlarged for legibility and its
transitional strokes use a deterministic simulation-time presentation phase.
