This replacement preserves recognizable coverall/goggle/respirator/blue-glove
and boot features; silhouette, cloth detail and authored gait are approximations.

The independent room-to-room wandering is a user-requested adaptation.
The original entity pursues the player after QPS collection; this one does not.
It is present at startup, cannot damage or physically block the fly, and may
cross walls as an apparition without changing their state. It has no authority
to observe/collapse maze walls. Only the fly's pose controls maze observation.
